I will never understand this. I have tried for years, but I just cannot understand what compels people to join this "sport".

To be fair, I am not exactly an expert in understanding why people get into sports to begin with. I've never really been around people who play sports recreationally. I know some people who follow professional sports, but its mostly old men.

I do understand competition. To a degree, anyway. With my arthritis, I can't actually participate in sports. But I compete in card games, board games, video games, etc, etc. It can be fun. Some people seem to get a lot more out of competition than I can understand. Or maybe they are just taking it personally, and staking their self worth on their ability to outperform someone else. Maybe I'm a beta male or something because I can always just say "its just a game" and move on with my life.

You know why soccer/football is so popular? Because it is so simple to play. Everyone knows that. But there are plenty of other sports that are more complicated and still gather huge followings.

Road cycling though? It sounds simple. It is just a race. How complicated can that be? Well, unlike other sports, road cycling is basically pay-to-win. Not only do you need to be an elite athlete to win a bike race but you have to be rich enough to afford the lightest and fastest bike that still falls within UCI regulations. And UCI regulates the ever living fuck out of these bikes.

That takes the fun out of it for me, but, apparently, not for a lot of people. As I write this, spring has just sprung, and the road cyclists have shaved their legs and covered themselves with the ugliest garb imaginable as they set out to conquer my commute once and for all. Or...no...that cant be right? What are they trying to do exactly?

Are they trying to win a race? Maybe I'm sure at least a few of them attend local races or something. But what would that prove? Are they gonna go pro or are they just trying to justify dropping 10k on a bike?

Are they trying to get in shape? I've met a lot who seem to see it that way but I never have the heart to ask them why they needed such an expensive setup. The thought "the lighter my bike is, the LESS calories I will burn on my ride" never seems to cross their mind.

Are they speed freaks? Do they simply want to push the limits of human powered travel? Feel in tune with the machine? Well that simply can't be true. If that were the case, they wouldn't be riding anything that looked remotely like a regular bike. They would at the very least be riding a recumbent, which is more aerodynamic, or they would even be riding a bike with fairings completely covering it. The human body, you see, is notoriously non-aero. But no, you never see anything like that blowing past you on the trail without even ringing their bell (bells aren't aero).

So all I am left with is the idea that this is all just a big larp. These people, of which there are apparently a lot of them, are just larping as elite athletes. This is the true and ultimate power of marketing: making you drop 10k on something that you not only don't need but will actively make everyone around you hate you in the process. Magic.
