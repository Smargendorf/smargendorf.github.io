It's true. I gave into the influencer marketing. Sam Seder fucking hawked some shit at me and I got sold a shiny new thing. I bought a thing BRAND NEW. What have I BECOME?? Im WEAK.

But I wanted to talk about this because it really fits the vibe of what I normally talk about: longevity and standards focused product design. No, I don't ONLY have opinions about bikes. Bikes only occupy, like, 80% of my thoughts. Don't discount that other 20%! There's some good(ish) stuff in there!

I've had a Henson razor for about 3 years at this point. My opinion is that modern reviews severly lack longevity factors in their ratings. When I review a product, I want that review to be backed up by as much wear and tear as possible. After 3 years, I think I have a pretty good idea of how much I like this razor. I like it. A lot.

I first heard about the Henson from a Majority Report ad read. I thought to myself "thats cool and all, but my electric razor works great, and I have no plans to spend $80 on a new razor that could slice my face open." This was cope.

When my electric razor eventually ran out of replacement heads that werent dull, I realized my mistake. I dont think anyone is under any illusions that consumer electric razors have standard head replacements. You gotta buy the propreitary shit. And guess what? They might not even make your razor anymore. Sure you dont want to drop $200 on an AI powered razor that tells you what the weather is and how big your dick is that day while youre shaving and has 10 minutes of battery life?

Safety razors use a standard razor blade. This standard has been THE standard for over a hundred years. Now THAT is a standard. New razor blade costs are measured in CENTS. No fucking wonder Gillette and the others moved to propriery plastic blade heads. Razor blades are simply TOO easy to produce and TOO universally accepted. We were letting that shit go on for WAY too long.

Anyway, I decided to see what the hype was about but didnt want to drop $80 on a razor just yet, so I bought a $10 safety razor off amazon. It was...fine. Definitely had to learnt o be extremely careful with it so that I didn't slice my face open. For a long time, I was kind of scared of shaving my face and ended up looking scraggly more often than usual. But I got used to it. I did some reading online and found that a lot of people actually did like the Henson razor saying it was foolproof and much easier to use than cheap razors, so I decided to "USE THE CODE MAJORITY AT CHECKOUT FOR A FREE 100 EXTRA RAZORS WITH YOUR PURCHASE" and boom, shiny new toy arrived at my door.

It works great. It does feel WAY harder to cut yourself. The shave feels consistant. It's easy to clean. I dont even use shaving cream any more, I just shave aftyer getting out of the shower and rub my face with warm water a couple times during the shave to keep it consistant. 10/10 experience. But clearly, that isnt what I, personally, love about it.

Even though safety razors are designed around a seemly immortal and universally good standard (no plastic in sight, universally compatible, AND cheap?? is this heaven on earth!?!?), lots of companies over the years have tried to make "improved" safety razors so they can justify buying a new thing when a good safety razor's selling point, to me, is it's simplicity. Weird hinge mechanisms, razor loading methods, attachments, etc. Until propriety cartriges were really embraced, companies were scrambling to improve something that was already basically perfect so they could convince you that you needed to buy a new version of a product you already had that should have lasted you generations.

THAT is where Henson stands apart for me. A henson razor is LITERALLY just 3 pieces of CNC machined aluminum. The entire razor is held together by the handle (which is threaded on the top end) going through one piece of the head and screwing into the other. If you think about it, the razor is TECHNICALLY just a bolt, a washer, and a nut. It doesnt get simpler than that, folks.

How does it hold up over time? It looks exactly the same as the day I bought it 3 years ago. Functions the same too. Those 100 razor I came with? Ive only gone through about 40 of them, if that. Fucking amazing. This thing will mostly likely outlive me. Couldn't ask for a better razor (they make a titanium version for the titanium-heads out there. it costs $300 and is heavier. Sounds like a downgrade, honestly).

Now, to be clear, other razor companies have extremely similar designs. Henson did not invent the 3 piece razor design, not even close. Hell, I'm sure Henson is overpriced for what it is. How else are they affording all those ad buys?? The point of this review is not to say that you should buy a Henson razor. But I AM saying you should buy a nice safety razor (of which Henson is certainly a contender) and never worry about running out of charge mid shave ever again.
